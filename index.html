Chelsea's number 10

Cold Palmer.

Cole Palmer is an English attacking midfielder and winger who joined Chelsea from Manchester City in September 2023. Known for his composure, creativity and trademark cold celebration, he quickly became a major figure for the Blues.

In October 2026, Chelsea announced that Palmer had signed a new contract running until 2034.
